## What this notice covers

This notice explains how we handle information about our business clients and prospective clients, and about the owners, staff and other people who use our client portal for them. It covers our intake, our setup and coaching services, our client portal, and our website.

Our services are for businesses. This notice does not cover the accounts you hold with other companies, such as AI, scheduling, invoicing or email providers. Those companies' own privacy policies and terms apply to those accounts.

Our Starter Terms, and any Master Services Agreement and Statement of Work we sign with you, control if they conflict with this notice.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- Contact details: names, job titles, business email addresses and phone numbers.
- Business details: your business name, services, service area, workflows, the tools you use (by name), approval rules and limits.
- Intake answers, portal messages, questions, approvals and requests.
- Files and other uploads you share with us.

**Information we create while working with you**

- Research notes about your business, with their sources and dates.
- Deployment prompts, drafts, ideas, plans and other working files.
- Context that AI assistants we use keep about your project, such as notes and memory.
- Transcripts or recordings of calls, only after we tell you at the start of the call. You may say no.

**Information collected when you use the portal**

- Account details: your email, your role, and sign-in and verification events. Your portal password is stored in protected (hashed) form by our sign-in provider. We cannot see it.
- Acceptance and signing records: the versions and digital fingerprints of the documents you accepted or signed, the date and time, your IP address, and browser and device information.
- Security logs, such as sign-in attempts and rate limits.

**Information from public sources**

Before we first contact you and during our work, we may research your business using public and business sources, such as your website, your online listings and your public reviews. We do not look for sensitive personal information, such as health details, financial accounts, government ID numbers or information about children. When we pre-fill portal fields from research, we show the source and date and ask you to confirm or correct them. We do not treat unconfirmed information as fact.

**Accounts you connect yourself**

We may suggest that you connect accounts, including bank or other financial accounts, through an official connector or integration offered by your software or AI provider or by a third-party data service. You connect them directly with that provider and sign in on its own screen. We never see or receive your logins, and we do not store full account or routing numbers. The provider handles that information under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share information from a connected account with us, we use it only to give you the help you asked for. You can disconnect at any time.

**Information we see on your screen**

If you share your screen or give us remote control during a session, we may briefly see information on your screen, including information about your customers or staff. We use what we see only to do the work you ask for, and we do not copy it unless the work requires it. Please close anything we do not need to see.

## Information we do not want

We will never ask for, and you should never send us: passwords for your other accounts, login credentials, passkeys, one-time or two-factor codes, recovery codes, API keys or access tokens, card numbers, bank account or routing numbers, Social Security numbers, or other account numbers. Please also do not send medical records, full mailbox or cloud-drive exports, or other people's sensitive personal information.

When we send you our own verification code for signing or a data request, enter it only in the portal's verification field. Never send it to us. We do not keep raw verification codes.

If you send us something on this list, we will delete it, not use it, and ask you to change it.

## Information about your customers and staff

Your business decides what information about your customers and staff goes into your own tools, and you are responsible for giving them any notices and getting any consents they are owed. When information about them reaches us, such as in a file you share or on a screen during a session, we use it only to do the work you asked for, keep it confidential, and delete it on the schedules below.

## How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as these schedules allow, unless a legal hold or the law requires us to keep a specific item longer.

- **Contract Record:** signed and accepted terms, receipts, acceptance and signing records, statements of work, invoices, subscription records, an admin log of actions without content (including proof of deletions by category and date), and messages that are approvals, notices or change requests. Kept throughout our relationship and for six years after the later of the end of the last statement of work or the final payment.
- **Intake materials,** call transcripts and recordings, and research notes, AI memory and working files built from your intake: deleted at the earlier of 24 months after your intake date or 90 days after Close. If you complete a new or updated intake, the 24 months for what it covers runs from that later date.
- **If no statement of work is ever signed:** intake materials are deleted 90 days after we decline or you withdraw, and in any case no later than 12 months after you submit the intake. An updated intake does not extend this deadline for an earlier submission.
- **Other portal records and messages:** deleted within 90 days after Close.
- **Research notes, AI memory and working files created later** that do not contain intake materials: deleted within 90 days after Close.
- **Files and uploads:** deleted within 90 days after Close, unless part of the Contract Record.
- **Copies held by service providers:** we delete them, or ask the provider to delete them, on the same schedule and record the result. Providers' own retention and backups follow their terms.
- **Our backups:** age out within 35 days. If we ever restore from a backup, we apply deletions again.
- **Routine security and rate-limit logs:** 30 days.

You can ask us to delete research notes, AI memory and working files, intake materials, and other records sooner, except for the Contract Record and items under a legal hold.

"Close" means the date your last active engagement with us ends. That is the later of the end of your last follow-up session (or termination, if there is no follow-up) and the end of your last active statement of work or Clockworks subscription.

## Account status

**Suspended** means a statement of work or Clockworks subscription is still active, but portal access is paused. Your information is kept on its normal schedule, and Close is not triggered. Suspension does not extend any deletion deadline.

**Archived** means no statement of work or Clockworks subscription is active, so Close has happened. You then have 90 days of read-only access to view and download your files and receipts. After that, portal access ends and deletion follows the schedules above. You can still get receipts during the six-year Contract Record period and make privacy requests by email.

## Your choices and requests

You can ask us for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion. Use "Request my data" in the portal, or email parker@mnclockworks.com. You do not need to be signed in.

- **Who can ask.** The business owner, or an officer named in signed records or designated by the owner in writing, may make business-wide requests and approve business-wide deletion. Other users may request their own messages and uploads. We send other users' business-wide requests to the owner.
- **How we verify.** We send a fresh code to the account email and confirm the requester's authority. We may ask for more information if we have doubts. We will never ask for ID documents, passwords or other secrets to verify a request.
- **Timing.** We respond within 30 days of receiving a request and aim to respond within 10 business days. If a request is complex or we receive many, we may extend once by up to 30 more days. We will tell you in writing before day 30, with the reason and the new deadline.
- **Results.** For each item we report "Deleted"; "Kept," with the reason, such as Contract Record, legal hold, or a backup that ages out by a stated date; or "Requested from provider, not confirmed," which means we asked the provider to delete it but cannot confirm that they did. We keep a minimal record of each request so we can show how it was handled and avoid restoring deleted information.
- **What we leave out of copies.** We remove other people's personal information, other clients' information, legal-privileged material, security secrets and trade secrets, and we tell you the categories removed. We never export passwords, security answers, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, health insurance or medical ID numbers, or biometric data. If we find any, we tell you the type and delete it.
- **If we say no.** If we decline any part of a request, we explain why and how to ask us to reconsider. Email us to ask for reconsideration, and we will tell you the outcome.

If a security incident involves your business's information, we will notify you promptly and as the law requires.
