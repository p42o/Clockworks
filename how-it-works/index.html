The steps

Here is exactly how it goes.

A phone call, an optional assessment, then a screen-share setup. You watch it get built, test it with me, and walk away knowing how to use it.

Get my free 15-minute phone audit

The steps, in order.

  1. 1

    Free 15-minute phone audit

    Call (763) 296-9777. Iris, our AI phone assistant, takes your details for Parker. We look at missed calls, quiet quotes, open invoices, review asks, and the questions you answer over and over.

  2. 2

    Optional $50 online presence assessment

    The online presence assessment is $50. We check how your business shows up across search, AI assistants, and ratings and review sites. You get the exact searches and prompts we ran, plus an interactive report showing where you showed up that day and what we'd recommend improving. It's a snapshot, since search results and AI answers change on their own, but it gives you a baseline to compare against later.

  3. 3

    $499 one-time deployment, done together over screen share

    We do it together on a call with screen share, and video if you like. We set up your AI team with you, test it together, and walk you through how to use it. You watch it get built.

  4. 4

    It runs on your own account

    Your business information, your instructions and the content we write for you are yours, and your AI team runs on your own platform account.
